# GTM Strategy Frameworks

3 original B2B GTM frameworks: EPIC, IMPACT, CRAFT, and the 4 Deal Execution skills.

## Skills
- **gtm-motion-picker**: Scores 4 GTM motions (E/P/I/C) and recommends which should lead
- **positioning-and-messaging**: Anchor Market + Craft Message from the 6-step IMPACT framework
- **prompt-improver**: Structure AI prompts with Character, Result, Artifact, Frame, Timeline
- **deal-review-and-close-plan**: Deal health score from the seller's own facts, ranked risks, dated next actions, a mutual close plan and a 30, 60 and 90 day account plan
- **discovery-and-buying-group**: Discovery questions in the seller's framework and a buying group map
- **negotiation-and-champion**: The cost of each concession, a trade for each, and an internal asset in the champion's own voice
- **sales-sequences**: Outbound and deal stage email sequences with send days set by script
